Privacy Policy

Agencia AI — Last updated: May 10, 2026

Agencia AI ("we", "us", or "our") operates a marketing automation platform that helps businesses manage and publish content on social media platforms. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your Facebook or Instagram account, we collect:

  • Your Facebook Page name and Page ID
  • A Page Access Token issued by Facebook to allow publishing on your behalf
  • Basic profile information (name, email) if provided during onboarding

We do not collect personal financial information, passwords, or sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Publish posts, images, and videos to your Facebook Page on your behalf
  • Read your Page's performance insights (likes, reach, engagement)
  • Display your connected accounts in our dashboard

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Facebook Data

We access your Facebook data using the Facebook Platform APIs in accordance with Meta's Platform Terms. Specifically, we request:

  • pages_manage_posts — to publish content on your Page
  • pages_read_engagement — to read post performance metrics
  • pages_show_list — to display your managed Pages for selection

Access tokens are stored securely and are only used to perform actions explicitly authorized by you. You can revoke access at any time from your Facebook App Settings.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in a secure database (Supabase) hosted on infrastructure with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Access tokens are stored encrypted and access is limited to authenticated system components only.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you disconnect your Facebook account or request deletion, we remove your access tokens and associated data within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Disconnect your Facebook account from our platform
  • Request deletion of all your data by contacting us
  • Revoke app permissions directly from your Facebook settings

7. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — for social media publishing
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — for content generation assistance

Each service is governed by its own privacy policy.
